🎓 Student Hub Portal

🌟 About Student Hub Portal

📖 Introduction

Student Hub Portal is an online system designed to help students manage their academic activities in one place. It provides quick access to attendance, courses, assignments, results, fees, library services, certificates, placement information, notices, events and academic calendar.

✨ Features

📊 Student Dashboard
📅 Attendance Management
📚 Course Details
📝 Assignment Submission
🏆 Result & Performance
💳 Fee Details
📖 Library Services
📢 Notices & Events
🗓 Academic Calendar
💼 Placement Information
👤 Profile & Settings

🎯 Objective

The main objective of the Student Hub Portal is to provide students with a single platform where they can access all academic information quickly and efficiently.

The portal reduces the need to visit multiple systems for different academic activities. Students can easily check their academic information, manage their activities and stay updated with important college information.
